## DRAFT ONLY

This Plan is currently only a DRAFT and has not been accepted or implemented by WICEN WA. It is largely incomplete.

## Why this page exists

The rest of this plan describes *how* WICEN WA communicates. It doesn't yet say *what for, at what scale, or in what priority order* — because those are Aims questions, and Aims are a committee decision, not something to be assumed while drafting the operational detail.

This page exists to surface that gap as a concrete set of questions, so the committee has something specific to discuss rather than an open-ended "what should WICEN WA's aims be?" Each cluster below feeds the next: **Aims decide scope → scope decides which [PACE domains](./domain-summary) get finished first → that decides which capabilities are actually load-bearing → that decides what the [Capability Self-Assessment](/self-assessment) and [Training](./training) pages should test and teach.** Right now that chain runs backwards — the plan has state-wide ambition built into it by default, without the committee having decided that's the aim.

## What's already implicit, scattered across the site

- The [About](/about) page's Mission is broad and generic — "support government agencies, emergency services, and the community during times of emergency, disaster, or special need." It doesn't say *which* agencies, at *what* scale, or in *what* priority.
- The homepage states WICEN WA is **"in a rebuilding phase"** — a real capacity constraint the PACE plan doesn't currently reflect anywhere.
- The [Capability Self-Assessment](/self-assessment) already encodes a working capability model (Physical, Mental Readiness, Radio & Equipment, Availability) and a set of operational roles (Field HF Operator, Net Control, Winlink/Digital Operator, APRS Tracker, etc.), and references **AIIMS** (the incident management system WICEN operates under) — none of which is referenced anywhere else in this plan.
- Recent WICEN WA activity (Dementia Walk, POTA activations, community events) is small-scale, local, single-day. This plan as drafted is written at full state-wide disaster scale — interstate liaison, regional HF gateways, statewide nets. That's a real gap between the organisation's current lived activity and the plan's ambition.
- Local/Tactical Field Comms (MeshCore) doesn't appear anywhere in the Capability Self-Assessment's model — so the assessment tool and this plan are currently describing two different pictures of what WICEN WA does.

## Questions for the team

### 1. Mission & scope

- Is WICEN WA aiming to rebuild toward full state-wide disaster-response capability, or toward being reliably excellent at local/regional community-event and moderate-incident support first, with state-wide capability as a later stage?
- Given the "rebuilding phase," what's a realistic 12–24 month capability ceiling — and should this plan document *current* capability, *target* capability, or both, clearly labelled as which?

### 2. Served agencies

- Which agencies does WICEN WA actually have, or want, a standing relationship with — DFES, SES, WA Police, Red Cross, local government? The [Deployment Model](./deployment-model) page currently just says "Supported Agency" generically.
- Do any of those relationships already dictate a comms platform or reporting format WICEN has to interoperate with, rather than choose?

### 3. Domain priority

- Of the five domains in the [Domain Summary](./domain-summary) — Activation & Alerting, Strategic Coordination, Long-Haul Message Traffic, Local/Tactical, Cross-State Coordination — which should be finished and drilled first, given current membership numbers?
- Is Cross-State Coordination a near-term priority at all, or aspirational for a later, larger membership?

### 4. Capabilities & training

- Should the [Capability Self-Assessment](/self-assessment) be extended to cover MeshCore/Local-Tactical now, or only once that domain's doctrine is settled?
- What's the minimum role set WICEN WA needs covered at all times (e.g. one Net Control, one Winlink operator) versus roles that are "nice to have when available"?
- Does the organisation want a formal training *pathway* (newbie → intermediate → advanced, tied to the roles already listed in the Capability Self-Assessment), or does the current quarterly ad-hoc [Training](./training) schedule remain sufficient at current size?

### 5. Testing what matters

- Should PACE domain readiness be tested at exercises the same way individual capability is self-assessed — i.e. does a domain's "Contingency" tier only count as real once it's been exercised, not just documented?

## Outcome

Answers to these questions should feed back into the [Overview](./overview) and [Domain Summary](./domain-summary) pages as explicit scope and priority statements, not stay only on this page. Until the committee has worked through them, treat the rest of this plan's scale and priority ordering as a draft assumption, not a decision.
