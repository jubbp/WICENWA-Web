## National context

Interstate and Commonwealth disaster coordination at the government level runs through the [Australian Government Crisis Management Framework](https://www.pmc.gov.au/resources/australian-government-crisis-management-framework-agcmf) and [COMDISPLAN](https://www.nema.gov.au/sites/default/files/2025-07/Australian%20Government%20Disaster%20Response%20Plan%202025%20(COMDISPLAN).pdf) (the Australian Government Disaster Response Plan), both administered by the National Emergency Management Agency (NEMA). WICEN WA's interstate liaison with other WICEN entities is a separate, amateur-radio-level relationship — it doesn't plug into COMDISPLAN directly — but it's worth the committee understanding the government-level mechanism this domain sits alongside, particularly if WICEN WA is ever asked to support a Commonwealth-coordinated response.
