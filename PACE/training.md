## Training and Exercises

Regular exercises ensure operational readiness.

Recommended schedule:

Quarterly radio exercises

Annual statewide activation exercise

Digital messaging training sessions

## Membership and what's expected

WICEN WA's Constitution (adopted at the 2026 AGM) has two classes of membership.

| | Ordinary member | Associate member |
|---|---|---|
| **Who** | A licensed amateur operator, or someone holding a qualification the committee accepts | Anyone who has registered an interest in supporting WICEN WA's activities. Members under 18 can only be associates |
| **Fee** | Annual membership fee, by the due date the committee sets | No fee |
| **Voting and office** | Votes at general meetings; can stand for the committee | No vote; can't hold office |
| **Activities** | Any role they are trained for | Any role they are trained for |

Membership class is about governance — voting and fees. It doesn't decide what a member can do in the field. That depends on training: every role needs the units listed for it, and radio roles need an amateur licence.

### What WICEN WA expects of every member

1. **Complete the induction (F1)** within three months of joining, and before taking part in any event, exercise or deployment. Meetings, nets and training sessions are open before then.
2. **Complete the Capability Declaration** at the induction, and send a quick update whenever your contact details, licence, equipment or availability change.
3. **Confirm your details once a year** at the pre-season readiness check {% include gap.html id="GAP-037" %}.
4. **Follow the code of conduct** {% include gap.html id="GAP-055" %} and acknowledge it on joining.
5. **Wait to be tasked.** Never self-deploy (see [Activation](./activation)).
6. **Train for the role before taking it.** You are only tasked to a role once you hold its units, and you stay in that role while you stay current.

Taking part in at least one WICEN activity a year — an event, exercise or training session — is encouraged. It isn't a condition of membership, but members who aren't current aren't tasked to field roles until they have refreshed.

### What members can expect from WICEN WA

- **Training for the role,** assessed and recorded, and a personal training plan showing what to do next.
- **A safety briefing** at every exercise, event and deployment. You are never asked to work beyond your training, you can decline any tasking, and anyone can stop an activity for a safety concern.
- **Straight answers about insurance.** WICEN WA holds public liability insurance, which also protects members acting for WICEN WA against claims by other people. It doesn't cover your own injuries {% include gap.html id="GAP-029" %}.
- **Your personal details kept private,** held only by the committee members who need them and never sent over the air.
- **No cost to volunteer** beyond the membership fee. WICEN WA never charges for its services and no member is paid; donations go to WICEN WA, not to individuals.

## Operator baseline

A code of conduct for members is not yet adopted {% include gap.html id="GAP-055" %}.

Every operator should know WICEN's [Voice Procedure (RATEL)](./voice-procedure) — calls and answers, prowords, spelling and numbers, relay, and passing a formal message — before taking a net or field role. It's the procedure every voice tier in the plan uses.

A standard field go-kit list is not yet defined {% include gap.html id="GAP-016" %}, and nor is a power standard {% include gap.html id="GAP-026" %}.

Agency access and entry requirements — insurance, inductions, ID, fireground training and protective clothing — are not yet defined {% include gap.html id="GAP-029" %}.

Preparing for non-amateur users (UHF CB, marine VHF) is not yet covered {% include gap.html id="GAP-039" %}.

## Running an exercise

Every exercise, and every field deployment, starts the same way:

1. **Conditions check.** The exercise controller checks the fire danger rating, any Total Fire Ban, the weather and the sites, and makes a go / no-go decision.
2. **Sign-in.** Every participant signs the Operator Sign-In Sheet, confirming they are fit for duty, and signs out before leaving.
3. **Safety briefing.** The safety officer briefs everyone from the Safety Briefing and Activity Safety Assessment, adds any site-specific hazards, and each participant signs the briefing record.

Exercise traffic starts and ends with **EXERCISE** {% include gap.html id="GAP-056" %}. A real emergency during an exercise is called with **NO DUFF**, which stops all exercise traffic. Anyone can stop the exercise for a safety concern.

Both forms are printable annexes of the WICEN-WA Operations Manual.
