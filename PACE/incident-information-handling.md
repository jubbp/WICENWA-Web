Example:

https://wicenwa.org/incident

This page should contain:

Current activation status

Operational frequencies

Net schedules

Situation reports

Deployment instructions

## Relationship to WA's Emergency Public Information arrangements

Western Australia's actual governing document for public warnings and information during an emergency is the [State Support Plan – Emergency Public Information](https://www.wa.gov.au/government/publications/state-support-plan-emergency-public-information), maintained by the State Emergency Management Committee (SEMC). This page describes a WICEN-internal information page, not that state-level function — and WICEN WA doesn't currently appear in the State Support Plan itself. See [Committee Discussion](./committee-discussion) §2 for the open question of whether WICEN WA should seek formal inclusion there.
