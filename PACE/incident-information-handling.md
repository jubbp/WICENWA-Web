Once WICEN is On Watch or Activated, members need the latest operational information in one place they can rely on: WICEN's status, the nets and frequencies in use, situation updates and deployment instructions.

How that information is distributed to members is not yet decided {% include gap.html id="GAP-059" %}. A public incident status page on the WICEN WA website was considered and dropped: keeping it current needs website skills and access the coordinator may not have during an incident, and it would publish operational detail to anyone.

Rules for relaying official public warnings are not yet defined {% include gap.html id="GAP-031" %}.

## Relationship to WA's Emergency Public Information arrangements

Western Australia's actual governing document for public warnings and information during an emergency is the [State Support Plan – Emergency Public Information](https://www.wa.gov.au/government/publications/state-support-plan-emergency-public-information), maintained by the State Emergency Management Committee (SEMC). This chapter is about WICEN's own information to its members, not that state-level function — and WICEN WA doesn't currently appear in the State Support Plan itself. {% include gap.html id="GAP-014" %}
