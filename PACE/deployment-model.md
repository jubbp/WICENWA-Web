Typical field deployment structure.

Incident Command
     |
Communications Unit
     |
Field Communications Teams
     |
Supported Agency

This hierarchy follows the shape of [AIIMS](https://www.afac.com.au/AIIMS) (the Australasian Inter-Service Incident Management System, published by AFAC and used by every fire, emergency, and land management agency in Australia) without currently saying so — the [Capability Self-Assessment](/self-assessment) already states WICEN operates under AIIMS, but this is the only page where that structure actually shows up. See [Committee Discussion](./committee-discussion) §2 for the open question of whether WICEN WA has any formal, agency-recognised place within this structure at all, or whether that's still informal.
