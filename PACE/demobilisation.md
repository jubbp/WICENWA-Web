## DRAFT ONLY

This procedure is a draft and has not yet been adopted, or agreed with any supported agency {% include gap.html id="GAP-034" %}.

## Where this fits in the plan

Every activation ends with demobilisation: WICEN WA stands down its stations, hands back what belongs to the agency, gets its people home safely, and returns to **Stand By** (see [Activation](./activation)). A deployment isn't finished until every member is home and accounted for.

Demobilisation planning starts at activation, not at the end — the coordinator should know from the start who will be released first, how far each team has to travel, and what equipment has to come back.

## When demobilisation starts

WICEN WA is demobilised when:

- the controlling agency releases WICEN, or no longer needs WICEN communications;
- for a planned event, race control releases WICEN — see [Event Support](./event-support); or
- the State Coordinator decides WICEN can no longer safely sustain the task — for example, not enough rested operators, or conditions have become unsafe.

WICEN never stands down a tasked service without first telling the agency or event organiser it is supporting and agreeing a release time.

## Standing down a station or net

1. **Agree the release time** with the agency's communications unit or WICEN's liaison (for an event, with race control).
2. **Clear open traffic.** No message is left unsent or unacknowledged — pass anything outstanding to the agency or to a station that is staying on, and log it.
3. **Close the net.** Net Control announces the closure time and any nets that are continuing, then makes the final log entry.
4. **Finish the records.** Complete and sign station logs, net logs and message forms; for an event, the logs go to the organiser {% include gap.html id="GAP-033" %}.
5. **Account for equipment.** Return agency equipment and record it as returned; pack WICEN and personal kit; note anything damaged or lost.
6. **Sign out** — with the agency, if signed in on arrival, and with the WICEN coordinator.

## Getting people home safely

- Nobody drives home straight after a long shift. Rest first {% include gap.html id="GAP-027" %}.
- Every member reports their safe arrival home to the coordinator, who keeps a list until everyone is accounted for.

## After stand-down

- **Hot debrief** before teams leave: what worked, what didn't, and any safety issues.
- **Welfare follow-up** with each member within a few days. Anyone affected by what they saw or did can also call Lifeline on 13 11 14.
- **Restore readiness** — recharge batteries, replace consumables, repair kit and restock 72-hour kits, so WICEN is ready for the next activation.
- **Formal debrief** within about two weeks, with a short report to the supported agency. Lessons are fed back into this plan {% include gap.html id="GAP-035" %}.
- **Return to Stand By.** The State Coordinator returns WICEN to Stand By, tells members on every alerting channel, and updates the [incident page](/incident).
