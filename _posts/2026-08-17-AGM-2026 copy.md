WICEN WA held its annual general meeting for 2026 on Monday 17th August 2026.

Thank you to the members who managed to attend either online or in person.

It was encouraging to see the continued growth in interest in emergency communications as we continue our efforts to rebuild WIDEN in Western Australia.

Your WICEN WA committee was elected as follows:

- President - Mark Bastin VK6XLZ
- Treasurer - Bob Lockley VK6KW
- Secretary - Phillip Jubb VK6DEV
- Committee Members - Leo Mertens VK6THW & Kathy Suminer VK6KTS

We look forward to another productive year of rebuilding.
